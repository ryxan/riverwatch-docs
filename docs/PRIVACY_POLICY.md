# Privacy Policy for RiverWatch

**Last updated: July 10, 2026**

## Information We Collect

RiverWatch is designed with your privacy in mind. We collect minimal information:

### Location Data
- **Purpose**: To show nearby river monitoring stations on the map
- **Storage**: Location data is used temporarily and not stored on our servers
- **Permission**: You can deny location access; the app will still function

### Network Access
- **Purpose**: To fetch real-time river level and flow data from USGS and Environment Canada
- **Data**: No personal information is transmitted

### Local Storage
- **Favorites**: Saved stations are stored locally on your device only
- **Settings**: User preferences are stored locally

## Third-Party Services

RiverWatch uses the following third-party services:

### Google Maps
- Used for displaying the map interface
- Subject to [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

### USGS Water Services
- Used to fetch river monitoring data
- Public data service with no account required

### Environment and Climate Change Canada
- Used to fetch Canadian river monitoring data
- Public data service with no account required

## Data Sharing

We do not:
- Sell your data
- Share your data with third parties (except as required for core functionality via Google Maps)
- Track your usage beyond what's necessary for app functionality

## Children's Privacy

RiverWatch does not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

## Your Rights

You can:
- Deny location permissions
- Clear app data at any time through Android settings
- Uninstall the app to remove all local data

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted in this document.

## Contact

For questions about this privacy policy, contact: app.riverwatch@gmail.com
